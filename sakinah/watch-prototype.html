How the detection works

Six CoreMotion and HealthKit signals are fused on-device. No cloud, no account.

Wudu gesture model

  • Niyyah — sustained stillness (~4s, low userAccel & rotation) auto-advances
  • Washing × 3 — three rotationRate peaks > 3.5 rad/s with bursts of userAccel > 0.25 g
  • Users can tap Next if the detector disagrees

Salah posture state machine

  • Qiyām — gravityY < −0.55 (forearm horizontal, palm inward), still
  • Rukūʿ — altitude drops ~15 cm, gravityY < −0.7, gravityZ near zero
  • Sujūd — altitude drops ~25 cm, gravityZ < −0.75 (wrist face-up on the floor)
  • Jalsa / Tashahhud — seated, mid-altitude, gravityZ > +0.3
  • A posture must be held for ≥ 600 ms before it's accepted — filters transitions

Rakah & sajdah counting

  • Each sujūd within a rakah increments the sajdah counter (1 → 2)
  • Returning to qiyām after two sujūds advances to the next rakah with a double .success haptic
  • Rakah can be manually adjusted ±1 if you lose count

Dhikr reminder

  • Rolling baseline from 25th-percentile of recent HR (30-minute window)
  • Trigger: HR > baseline + 20 bpm and HRV SDNN < 30 ms
  • Cooldown: 15 minutes between suggestions — never naggy

Mosque finder

  • MKLocalSearch with natural-language query "mosque" within 3 km
  • Sorted by great-circle distance from current location
  • Proactively suggests nearest mosque when a prayer window opens within 10 minutes

Prayer times

  • Self-contained astronomical calculation — no network dependency
  • ISNA defaults, switchable to MWL / Umm al-Qura / Karachi / Egyptian General Authority
  • Asr factor toggles between Shafiʿi (×1) and Hanafi (×2)
